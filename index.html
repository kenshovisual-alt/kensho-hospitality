What makes Kensho different from other digital marketing agencies?

Kensho was built from direct hospitality experience, not just marketing theory. We have worked inside luxury hotels, restaurants and hospitality operations ourselves, so we understand the day-to-day pressures behind service, reservations, guest experience, staffing, reviews and operations. That understanding is combined with modern AI systems, SEO, content strategy and continuous research into hospitality trends and guest behaviour.

What type of hospitality businesses do you work with?

Restaurants, hotels, resorts, rooftops, beach clubs, spas, riads, concierge companies, hospitality groups, nightlife concepts, dinner shows and hospitality-focused agencies. Every strategy is adapted to the guest experience and audience each business is targeting.

Why are some restaurants and hotels appearing inside AI search results now?

AI platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity already influence how people choose restaurants, hotels and travel experiences. Businesses with stronger digital signals, structured information, reviews, authority and online visibility are more likely to appear in these recommendations. It is one of the biggest visibility shifts in hospitality.

Can AI actually help increase direct bookings and revenue?

Yes. AI tools automate guest communication, improve response times, optimise visibility and reduce operational friction, all of which can increase direct reservations and reduce dependence on OTAs.

Why is Google Business Profile so important for restaurants and hotels today?

For many hospitality businesses, the Google Business Profile is now as important as the website, sometimes more. Most guests search on Google Maps before deciding where to go, especially on mobile. Reviews, photos, opening hours, location, menu information and overall activity directly affect whether someone chooses you or your competitor.

Why do most hospitality social media accounts fail to generate real business?

Because most brands post without a clear positioning, visual identity or guest psychology behind the content. Random food photos and inconsistent posting rarely create desire or trust. Hospitality content should communicate atmosphere, status, emotion and experience, not just products.

Do you only focus on social media and advertising?

No. Kensho combines branding, SEO, GEO, websites, AI systems, Google visibility, automation, content production and operational tools into one hospitality growth strategy. Social media is only one part of the ecosystem.

Can you help reduce dependence on Booking.com and OTAs?

Yes. One of the main goals is increasing direct visibility and direct reservations through SEO, GEO, Google Business optimisation, stronger branding and better digital infrastructure.

Why do so many hospitality websites fail to convert visitors into bookings?

Most are outdated, slow, visually generic or badly structured for how guests behave today. Guests decide very quickly whether a place feels trustworthy, premium and worth booking. Small details in design, speed, visuals and structure heavily affect conversion.

Is AI replacing hospitality jobs?

No. In hospitality, AI works best as support infrastructure, not as a replacement for human service. The goal is to reduce repetitive tasks, improve response times and help teams work more efficiently while keeping the human experience intact.
